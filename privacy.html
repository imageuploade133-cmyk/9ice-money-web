Section 01 • Data Classification

1. Information We Collect

To provide robust financial settlements, mobile agency banking, and hardware POS operations, 9iceMoney collects specifically defined data categories governed by CBN regulations and NDPA mandates.

person_pin Identity & KYC Data
  • Legal full name, DOB, gender
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN) validation
  • National Identification Number (NIN) hash
  • Residential / Kiosk GPS physical address
account_balance_wallet Financial & Ledger Data
  • Virtual settlement account details
  • Transaction Reference Numbers (RRN/STAN)
  • Aggregated wallet float balance & histories
  • Utility bills & token recharge records
point_of_sale POS Hardware Telemetry
  • POS terminal serial number & firmware version
  • Cellular SIM IMSI / IMEI anti-fraud pairing
  • Kiosk geofence verification markers
  • Tamper sensor logs & diagnostic data
devices Technical & Log Streams
  • IP address & ISP network carrier
  • Device OS architecture & build fingerprint
  • App session token lifecycles
  • Biometric unlock challenge response tokens
Section 02 • Legal Foundation

2. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under Section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023), 9iceMoney processes user information solely under validated lawful grounds:

1. Contractual Necessity

Processing required to maintain your digital wallet, route card authorizations through NIBSS, remit agency commission floats, and dispatch physical POS replacement hardware.

2. Compliance with Statutory Obligations

Fulfilling directives of the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, and reporting to the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU).

3. Legitimate Interests & Fraud Prevention

Defending the agency platform against unauthorized chargebacks, botnet exploits, SIM-swap attacks, hardware physical tampering, and illicit float diversion.

Section 03 • Identity Governance

3. BVN & NIN Verification Protocols

Your Bank Verification Number (BVN) and National Identity Number (NIN) are used exclusively to confirm that you are the authentic account creator through CBN and NIMC sanctioned verification rails.

What We Access

Full Name, Date of Birth, Registered Phone Number, and verified photograph matched against identity databases.

What We NEVER Access

We never see or access your other commercial bank balances, external account login credentials, or external ATM debit card PINs.

Section 04 • Hardware Resilience

4. Smart POS & Terminal Data Security

9iceMoney operates thousands of Smart POS Android devices across agency networks. Every terminal is hardened against physical, acoustic, and cryptographic tampering.

If physical intrusion of the POS outer shell is detected, the cryptographic keys stored inside the processor's battery-backed memory instantly trigger a self-destruct wipe. Customer card PIN entries are encrypted immediately at the physical keypad level using DUKPT algorithms.
